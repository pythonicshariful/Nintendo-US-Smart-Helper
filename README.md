# Nintendo US Smart Helper 🎮

Welcome to the **Nintendo US Smart Helper**! This is a simple, automated bot that runs right in your browser. It helps you buy hard-to-get Nintendo products, like the new Nintendo Switch or limited edition controllers, before they sell out.

This guide is written so anyone can understand it—no coding experience required!

---

## 🚀 How to Use the Bot (Two Main Scenarios)

### Scenario 1: The product is ALREADY IN STOCK right now
If you see the product is in stock (or available for "Pre-order" / "Pre-purchase") but you want the bot to buy it for you super fast:

1. Go to the product's page on `nintendo.com`.
2. Open the bot panel (click the red **"N"** button in the bottom right corner).
3. Go to the **[SETTINGS]** tab and turn ON **"Enable Auto Checkout"**.
4. (Optional) Enter your Nintendo account password in the settings so the bot can automatically log you in during checkout if needed. Click **Save Settings**.
5. Click the green **"START"** button at the top of the bot panel.
6. **That's it!** The bot will instantly click "Add to Cart" and rapidly fly through all the checkout steps (Shipping, Payment, Review, and Purchase) faster than a human ever could.

### Scenario 2: The product is OUT OF STOCK
If the product is currently sold out and you are waiting for a restock:

1. Open the bot panel and go to the **[WATCH]** tab.
2. Copy the website link (URL) of the sold-out product and paste it into the bot.
3. Click **"Watch This Product"**.
4. Go to the **[SETTINGS]** tab and make sure **"Enable Auto Checkout"** is turned ON.
5. Click the green **"START"** button.
6. **Leave the tab open!** The bot will silently check Nintendo's secret stock system every few seconds in the background. 
7. **When it comes back in stock:** The bot will instantly detect it, jump to the product page, and buy it for you automatically!

---

## ⭐ Main Features

- **Lightning Fast Auto-Buy:** Automatically clicks through "Add to cart", "Continue to shipping", "Continue to payment", and "Purchase".
- **Pre-order & Pre-purchase Support:** It doesn't just look for "Add to cart"—it completely supports buying "Pre-order" and "Pre-purchase" items too!
- **Background Stock Watcher:** You can add multiple sold-out products to the Watchlist. It checks them all at once in the background without refreshing your page.
- **Auto Re-login:** If Nintendo asks for your password right before finalizing the purchase, the bot can type it in for you (if you saved it in the Settings) so you don't lose the item.
- **Price Drop Alerts:** Looking for a discount? Set a "Target Price" in the settings, and the bot will notify you with an on-screen alert and a sound if the price drops to your target!
- **Anti-Ban System:** The bot randomizes the time between stock checks (e.g., between 30 to 60 seconds) so Nintendo thinks you are a real human, preventing you from getting blocked.

## 🛠️ How to Install

1. Install the **Tampermonkey** extension for your browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).
2. Click the Tampermonkey icon in your browser toolbar and select **"Dashboard"**.
3. Click the **"+"** icon to add a new script.
4. Delete everything in the text box, and paste the code from the `Nintendo US - Smart Helper.js` file.
5. Go to File > Save (or press `Ctrl+S`).
6. Go to `www.nintendo.com/us/`. You should now see a red **"N"** button in the bottom right corner of your screen. Click it to open the bot!

---

## 💡 Quick Tips

* **Don't close the tab:** The bot only works while the Nintendo website is open in a browser tab. If you are watching for a restock, keep the tab open in the background!
* **Empty Watchlist:** If the bot is started but your Watchlist is empty, it will just wait on the current page for you to add something or to auto-buy the item on your screen.
* **Stop at any time:** If you want the bot to stop doing everything, just click the red **"STOP"** button in the bot panel.
